Company Background

Armor Air TN (DBA "Armor Air") is a residential HVAC and plumbing home-services company based in Murfreesboro, TN, serving Murfreesboro, Franklin, Nashville and surrounding rural Middle Tennessee. HubSpot lists ~40 employees. The company is a sister/related account to "Armor Air" in Indianapolis, IN (a separate, larger ~$7.5M/year HubSpot company record) — Ryan Yarusi is a silent owner in Indianapolis and an active operating owner in Tennessee, alongside partners James Robertson and others. Tennessee is on track for $2M+ this year with a goal of $3M.

They bought Avoca to replace/reduce reliance on their current ServiceTitan-native AI answering and a third-party after-hours call center, both of which were causing missed/mis-booked jobs, wrong customer data capture, and long call durations that led to hang-ups. Taylor (their CSR) was spending time "babysitting" the AI's mistakes. They're ramping marketing (new agency, direct mail, Google/Facebook ads) heading into cooling season and want a more reliable, faster, on-brand phone experience before call volume spikes. Per internal Slack (#kudos, 4/23), Armor Air was also enabled on Avoca Outbound and Speed-to-Lead, with positive early feedback from the account team.
