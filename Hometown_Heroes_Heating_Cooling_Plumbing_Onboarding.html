Company Background

Hometown Heroes Heating, Cooling, & Plumbing (operating as "Hometown Heroes KC," hometownheroeskc.com) is a residential-focused HVAC and plumbing company serving the greater Kansas City metro (50-mile radius from Raytown, MO), including Lee's Summit, Independence, Overland Park, and Kansas City KS/MO. Founded in 2021 by Andrew Hay on a "local first," relationships-over-revenue philosophy. Roughly 10 employees per HubSpot, with 4 field technicians (HVAC + plumbing) and a lean office of 1–2 CSRs (Tina primary) per Attention calls. They run 35–40 calls/day on busy days (~600/month), sourced almost entirely from Google LSA after dropping ANGI/Thumbtack/other aggregators for poor performance.

They bought Avoca primarily to fix a broken after-hours experience ("Answering KC" was described as "rough" with dropped calls) and to relieve an overworked, single-point-of-failure CSR (Tina) heading into peak summer season. Leadership (Kirk, Rob, Andrew) evaluated Avoca roughly 3 years ago and felt the AI "wasn't there yet" / was "very robotic" — they checked references before re-engaging this time and were reassured. They explicitly want a conservative, phased rollout (Responder → Outbound → Speed to Lead) rather than turning everything on at once, and value the 3-month cancellation policy as a safety net.
